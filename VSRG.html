VSRG (Vertical scrolling rhythm games)

I really like rhythm games, specially of these kind. Usually, In a VSRG the notes come from the top to the bottom of the screen, and you need to click the respective button when it reaches the receptor.

Probabily you already heard of games like Guitar Hero, Project Sekai, Osu mania and Robeats. All of those are VSRG!

The game that I play the most is Osu! mania, you can find my profile here. But sometimes I also play other games of the genre, like Etterna, quaver and robeats.
